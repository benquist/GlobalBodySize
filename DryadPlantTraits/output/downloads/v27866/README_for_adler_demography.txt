Description of column headers in adler_demography.csv

species: species name (Latin binomial)
growthForm: description of species' growth form 
dimensions: dimension of transition matrix		
lambda: asymptotic population growth rate 	
SurvivalV: mean survival rate	
SurvivalS: mean sensitivity of lambda to survival	
SurvivalEraw: raw elasticity of lambda to survival	
RecruitV: mean fecundity 	
RecruitS: mean sensitivity of lambda to fecundity
RecruitEraw: raw elasticity of lambda to fecundity
GrowthTotV: mean individual growth rate	
GrowthTotS: mean sensitivity of lambda to individual growth
GrowthTotEraw: raw elasticity of lambda to individual growth
TotElas: the sum of the three raw elasticities	
SurvivalE: scaled survival elasticity	
RecruitE: scaled fecundity elasticity		
GrowthTotE: scaled grwoth elasticity		
kingdom: taxonomix kingdom
phylum: taxonomix phylum
AngioGymnosperm: Angiosperm or Gymnosperm
dicotMonocot: codes for dicot, Monocot, or NA
class: taxonomix class
order: taxonomix order
family: taxonomix family	
raunkier: alternate plant growth form description	
clonality: units of demographic resolution, either "Ramet" or "Genet:	
Country: nation
criteriaSize: if transition matrix structured by size then either "Yes" or unit of measurement, otherwise NA
criteriaStage: if transition matrix structured by stage then either "Yes" or description of stages, otherwise NA	
criteriaAge: if transition matrix structured by age then either "Yes" or description of age proxy, otherwise NA		
criteria: size (S), stage (ST) and or age (A)	
temporalReplication: number of years for which demographic data available	
start: starting year of data set	
end: ending year of data set	
spatialReplication: number of populations for which demographic data available		
ecoregion2: description of biome to which the species belongs
