Root system architectural data about seven legumes species growing in four nutrient supply treatment (four different volumetric concentrations: 0.025%, 0.05%, 0.1% and 0.0% (pure deionized water)) and measured in four time cuts (one, two, three and four weeks after seed germination). 

Each row corresponds to a single individual(column "plantID" - same for all four measurement) in given time (column "week"). 

Columns "width[px]","depth[px]","distance_from_top_of_root_system_to_centroid[px]","area[px2]","relative_depth","relative_width","length_of_main_root[px]","length_of_lateral_roots[px]","number_of_lateral_roots" and "mean_length_of_lateral_roots[px]" reported measured parameters of root systems (see Matherials and Methods part of paper for more details).

Column "seedmass[mg]" mean species-specifis average seed mass which was obtained by weighing 100 seeds(see Matherials and Methods part of paper for more details).
