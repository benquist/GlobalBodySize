DATA & FILE OVERVIEW
--------------------

File list (filenames, directory structure (for zipped files) and brief description of all data files):
	
	tundra_shrub_traits.csv : csv file including plant trait data measured in individuals of four arctic shrub species under experimental conditions. The permafrost thaw and fertilization experiment (Peng et al., 2017) ran for four years (2011-2014) in the nature reserve of Kytalyk, north-eastern Siberia (70°49'N, 147°28'E). The shrub species, dominant at the research site, were the deciduous species Betula nana ssp. exilis (Sukazcev) Hultén and Salix pulchra Cham., and the evergreen species Ledum palustre ssp. decumbens (Aiton) Hultén and Vaccinium vitis-idaea L.
	
	tundra_shrub_traits_README.txt: txt file including metadata of the dataset tundra_shrub_traits.csv

DATA-SPECIFIC INFORMATION 
--------------------------

Dataset: tundra_shrub_traits.csv

	Number of variables: 36

	Number of cases/rows: 720

	Missing data codes: NA

	Variable list:

		blk: experimental block (5 blocks: blk1, blk2,..., blk5)
		plt: experimental plots (30 plots: WE01, WE02,..., WE03)
		cable: indicates if a heating cable was installed in the plot (cY) or not (cN)
		heating: indicates if the permafrost thaw treatment was applied to the plot (hY) or not (hN)
		wtr: indicates if plots had heating cables or not and if the cables were heated or unheated (Ct: control (no cable), Ca: unheated cable, W: heated cable)
		ftr: indicates if nutrients were added to the plot (F) or not (nF)
		tr: indicates the combination of treatments applied to a plot (CtNF, CaNF, WNF, CtF, CaF, WF)
		PFT: shrub plant functional type (decid: deciduous, everg: evergreen)
		decidu: indicates if a shrub species is deciduous (dY) or not (dN)
		evergr: indicates if a shrub species is evergreen (eY) or not (eN)
		betula: indicates if a shrub belongs to the species Betula nana (Y) or not (N)
		salix: indicates if a shrub belongs to the species Salix pulchra (Y) or not (N)
		ledum: indicates if a shrub belongs to the species Ledum palustre (Y) or not (N)
		vacci: indicates if a shrub belongs to the species Vaccinium vitis-idaea (Y) or not (N)
		ind: code assigned to each individual; the first digit indicates the species(Sp1: V. vitis-idaea, Sp2: B. nana, Sp5: L. palustre, Sp8: S. pulchra) ant the last digits indicates the individual (01,...,06) 
		spp_abbr: species name abbreviation (Betn: B. nana, Ledp: L. palustre, Salp: S. pulchra, Vacv: V. vitis-idaea)
		tot_bio: total aboveground biomass (g) (dry weight)
		wood_bio: total aboveground wood biomas (g) (dry weight)
		leaf_bio: total leaf biomass (g) (dry weight)
		LDMC1: leaf dry mass content of leaf 1 (mg/g) 
		LDMC2: leaf dry mass content of leaf 2 (mg/g)
		LDMC: average leaf dry mass content (mg/g)
		LA1: leaf area of leaf 1 (cm^2)
		LA2: leaf area of leaf 2 (cm^2)
		LA: average leaf area (cm^2)
		SLA1: specific leaf area of leaf 1 (cm^2/g)
		SLA2: specific leaf area of leaf 2 (cm^2/g)
		SLA: average specific leaf area (cm^2/g)
		LNC: leaf nitrogen concentration (%)
		LPC: leaf phosphorus concentration (mg/g)
		CN_ratio: carbon:nitrogen ratio
		height: shrub height (cm)
		SSD: stem-specific density (g/cm^3)
		bark: bark thickness (mm)
		XD: xylem diameter (mm)
		SWC: stem water content (%)
