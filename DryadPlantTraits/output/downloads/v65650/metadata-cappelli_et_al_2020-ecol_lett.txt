Block	Block number; 1 to 4
Composition	Specific Species composition (81 levels)
Plot	Plot number
Nitrogen	Nitrogen treatment
Fungicide	Funcide treatment
Plant_richness	Plant species richness; 1, 4, 8, 20 Species
Functional_composition	Functional composition; slow growing (S), fast growing (F) or a mix of fast and slow growing species (M)
Sown_SLA	Mean specific leaf area of all sown species
SLA_shift	Difference between sown SLA and abundance weighted mean SLA
Micr_humidity	Mean difference between ambient % humidity and humidity in the vegetation (measured at different time points in the different plots)
Micr_temperature	Mean difference between ambient temperature and temperature in the vegetation (measured at different time points in the different plots)
Plant_cover	total plant cover
Infection_incidence	abundance weighted mean of % infected individuals per species
