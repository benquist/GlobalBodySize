This spreadsheet describes the sapwood water potential, relative water content and cumulative water released from sapwood samples of 32 plant species measured in a thermocouple psychrometer and their wood densities.
For each species up to 5 replicate branch samples were measured. 
For each branch sample a water relase curve was generated by successively drying branch samples and determining their relative weater content (RWC) and water potential at 4 or more intervals.

Cumulative water released (CWR, kg m-3) was calculated as:

CWR=(1-RWC)x(Ws-Wd)x((p x 1000)/Wd)


where p is wood density (g cm-3) determined from the volume of sapwood segments (calculated using the equation for volume of a cylinder from measurements of length and diameter of each segment), 
Ws is sapwood saturated weight determined after 24 h hydration, and Wd is sapwood dry weight determined after drying in an oven for more than 48 h at 60°C. 

In the worksheet labelled "raw data", the water release curve measurements are recorded. In the worksheet labelled "wood density", the wood density of branch samples of each replicate for each species is recorded.
