This dataset contains the leaf traits, plant size and environment data of 262 individuals of Clausena dunniana in the karst forests of Maolan Nature Reserve in China. There are 262 rows and 10 columns in total. Specifically,

The first four colums are leaf traits, in the order of specific leaf area, leaf area, leaf dry-matter content and leaf thickness.

The fifth column is plant size, which is the first principal component of stem basal diameter and plant height.

The sixth and seventh columns are canopy height and rock-bareness rate, respectively.

The eighth column is topographic habitat, includes two types, the top (top of the hill) and the slope.

The ninth column is the six study sites, represented by 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6.

The last column is the two study regions, represented by A and B.
