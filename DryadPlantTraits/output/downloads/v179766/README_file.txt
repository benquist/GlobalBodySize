Data from "Graminoids vary in functional traits, carbon dioxide and methane fluxes in a restored peatland: implications for modeling carbon storage" by Goud et al. 2022. 

Graminoid Plant Species

Beckmannia = American Slough Grass (Beckmannia syzigachne (Steud.) Fernald)
Calamagrostis = Bluejoint (Calamagrostis canadensis (Michx.) P. Beauv.)
Carex = Silvery Sedge (Carex canescens L.)
Eriophorum = Tussock Cottongrass (Eriophorum vaginatum L.)
Scirpus = Woolgrass (Scirpus cyperinus (L.) Kunth)

Plant traits and gas fluxes

NEP: Net ecosystem CO2 production, μmol CO2 m-2 s-1 
ER: Ecosystem respiration, μmol CO2 m-2 s-1
GPP: Gross primary productivity, μmol CO2 m-2 s-1
CH4: Methane, mg m-2 d-1
alpha: quantum efficiency, φ
Biomass: Aboveground biomass, g
Height: Maximum plant height, m
N: Leaf nitrogen percent element 
d13C: Leaf carbon stable isotope ratios 
d15N: Leaf nitrogen stable isotope ratios 
se: standard error of the mean
