Project Info:
“Shifts in woody plant defence syndromes during leaf development” published in Functional Ecology.

Data Table: Barton_Leaf_Development_Defence_Syndrome_Data.csv 

Description:
Effect size data for comparisons of defence traits in immature young leaves vs. mature leaves, analyzed with meta-analysis. 


Column headings and description: 

REFERENCE		Shortened citation of manuscript
PLANTFORM		Woody plant lifeform (tree or shrub)
PLANTFORM2		Evergreen or deciduous
FLUSH			Leaf phenology (synchronous or asynchronous, or both)
OLD.PLANTSPP		Plant species name as reported in the publication
PLANTSPP		Plant species name updated from The Plant List
FAMILY			Plant family
EXPERIMENT		Study site (greenhouse or field)
BIOGEOGRAPHY		Study site biogeography (tropics, temperate, boreal)
TRAIT			Highest order trait group (nutrients, physical, 					indirect, secondary chemistry)
TRAITCLASS		Second order trait group
TRAITMORE		Third order trait group (specific trait – phenolics, 				lignin, food body, nitrogen, etc.)
LRR			Log response ratio
LRR_VAR			Variance log response ratio
